TTT 410: Plastic Fantastic

Posted on Sunday, Mar 29, 2015 | Category: episodes | cyprus, eurovision-song-contest, san-marino, serbia

Show Notes

Society’s obsession with beauty is weird and getting them to do even weirder things - like getting fake abs, fake eyebrows, and fake dog testicles. Eric and Rachelle tackle plastic surgery, why people do it, and how they would do it. Speaking of plastic, we cover the entries from Cyprus, Serbia, and San Marino in this year;s Eurovision Song Contest.
