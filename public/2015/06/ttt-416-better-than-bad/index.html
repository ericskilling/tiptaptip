TTT 416: Better Than Bad

Posted on Tuesday, Jun 9, 2015 | Category: episodes | bad-art

Show Notes

Did you ever dislike something so much that you went full circle into liking it? In this episode we discuss “so bad they’re good” music, movies, and art. The Dirty Dancing Soundtrack, 1980s Saxophone solos, Tommy Wiseau’s The Room, Bad Art. What’s not to like?

We are Calgary’s only podcast!
