Staying in shouldn't be this much fun. And yet — two months later — here we are. Eric & Rachelle have gotten used to this new reality. Rachelle still wants chickens despite what our neighbours think.

In this episode we cover how the pandemic is affecting us and what we've done to cope. We check in on how the world is doing; cover some Eurovision stuff and go over some good work that some apple detectives are doing in the Pacific NW.
